## Library additions (November 2026: Christmas decor, more decor, wallpapers, floors)

Decor: Blender 5.1 (Cycles) renders by `assets-src/scripts/additions/render_additions.py` (it loads `library/render_library.py` and uses its camera rig, lights and helpers unchanged): front-on orthographic camera tilted 5 degrees down, transparent background, real size at 14 px per inch, WebP. Same method and licence basis as the decor library above. Textures: `scripts/additions/make_additions_textures.py`. Only CC0 sources; nothing here needs a credit line, so `CREDITS.md` and `assets/credits.json` are unchanged. No AI-generated art.
